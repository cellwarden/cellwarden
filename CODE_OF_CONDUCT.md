# Code of conduct

CellWarden follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the
work. Harassment or personal attacks are not accepted.

Report problems privately to the maintainers listed in AUTHORS. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainers may remove comments or contributors that break the code.
